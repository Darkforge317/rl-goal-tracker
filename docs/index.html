Plugin Features

Preset Goal Lists

Quick setup of common goals with built-in presets for Barrows gear, Void armor, and Ironman progression.

Pin Important Goals

Keep your top priorities easily accessible by pinning crucial goals directly to the top of your Home panel.

Automatic Prerequisites

Automatically add all required prerequisite tasks and sub-quests for your quests in a single click.

Visual Progress Bars

Track your real-time progress toward completion with clean, visual progress indicators for each goal.

JSON Import & Export

Seamlessly transfer your goal configurations between accounts, export backups, or share setups with friends.

Custom Goals & Tasks

Create personalized goals with custom task lists, right-click menu management, and item searching capabilities.
