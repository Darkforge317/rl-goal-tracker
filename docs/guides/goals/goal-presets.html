Goal Presets

Presets create ready-made goals with tasks already arranged for a particular checklist. They are a starting point: you can rename, edit, reorder, or remove the goals and tasks after adding them.

Where to Find Presets

On the Goal Tracker home panel, choose Add preset. Select a preset from the menu to see its description and how many goals and tasks it will add.

Add a Preset

  1. Choose Add preset.
  2. Select a preset and review its details.
  3. Confirm to add it to your goal list, or cancel to leave your goals unchanged.

After it is added, supported tasks are checked against your current RuneLite progress. Tasks that already meet their targets can appear complete immediately.

Available Presets

  • Early Ironman Progression, Mid Ironman Progression, and Late Ironman Progression: skill, item, and quest milestones for different stages.
  • Full Void Armor and All Barrows Gear: item collection checklists.
  • Fast Travel Unlocks: quests that unlock transportation methods.
  • Free-to-Play Quests: a quest checklist with a manual reminder to find a Shield of Arrav partner.
