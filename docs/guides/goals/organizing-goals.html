Organizing Goals

Use the home panel to keep your goals in a useful order and make frequently used goals easy to find.

Move Goals

Right-click a goal and open Move to move it up or down one position, or move it to the top or bottom of its list.

Pin Goals

Right-click a goal and choose Pin goal. Pinned goals are grouped at the top of the home list. Unpin a goal from the same menu; it returns to the unpinned group.

Rename Goals

Right-click a goal on the home panel and choose Rename, or open the goal and use Edit beside its name. Save to apply the change or cancel to discard it.

Delete Goals

Right-click the goal and choose Remove. Removing a goal also removes all of its tasks. Use the home panel’s Undo button to restore a recently removed goal, or Redo to remove it again.
