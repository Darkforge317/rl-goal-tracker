Deleting Tasks

Deleting a task also deletes all of its child tasks. If you want to keep those tasks, first move or detach them from the task you are removing.

Remove from the Context Menu

  1. Right-click the task.
  2. Choose Remove.

Quick Delete

Hold Shift and left-click the task row to remove it and its descendants. Release Shift when you are done; while it is held, row clicks are in delete mode.

Undo a Removal

Use the goal view’s Undo button to restore a recently removed task subtree. Redo reapplies the removal.
