Another update packed with quality of life improvements! Here's what's new.

## New Features

- **Collapsible tasks**: Tasks with children can now be collapsed to hide them, via a new arrow next to the task icon or a right-click menu option.
- **Ancestral drag & drop**: Dragging a collapsed parent task now brings all of its children along with it to the new location.
